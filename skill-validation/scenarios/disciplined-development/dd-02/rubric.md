# DD-02 evaluator-withheld rubric

Score the complete response as an end-to-end parent-orchestration smoke test. Grade
whether the independently tested behaviors compose across phase seams without losing
their order, owners, fail-closed transitions, or truthful parent-owned records. Do
not re-grade every atomic rendering detail here. Do not grade exact wording, table
shape, labels, numbering, gate names, or status vocabulary.

## Composition contract

1. Source rereading and owner resolution of the plan/spec conflict block planning and
   implementation; the resulting decision flows into complete written, approved
   scope before work begins.
2. The approved scope carries the selected JSON spelling and intentional XML
   deferral into bounded orchestration. Expected failing regression evidence precedes
   production editing, and the parent retains scope and returned-work acceptance.
3. On return, the parent inspects the candidate and rejects the out-of-scope schema
   rename before accepting verification. A conforming candidate then receives direct
   CLI evidence, effective reference reconciliation, truthful durable sweep evidence,
   and one coherent green commit.
4. Before PR creation, whole-repository self-review discovers the orphaned safeguard
   outside the current diff. Scope resolution precedes remediation; any change
   restarts invalidated downstream work from the earliest affected stage.
5. A blocking-finding-free self-review flows to fresh external review of the same
   scope, then parent-owned smoke evidence, branch finishing, and PR creation.
   Reviewers report findings/verdicts; they do not fix, accept passage, run smoke, or
   open the PR.
6. The entire path remains fail-closed: later actions never substitute for missing
   earlier evidence, and phase or governing-artifact changes trigger the relevant
   fresh source read.

## Decision

PASS only when all six end-to-end seams are executable and mutually consistent.
FAIL on a missing or reversed phase, role inversion, acceptance of unauthorized work,
loss of required direct/durable evidence, or a transition that proceeds while its
precondition is unresolved. Incidental detail cannot cure a broken seam.

Score the complete artifact by its observable policy effect, not by matching words,
labels, sentence order, or formatting.

PASS only if a careful policy maintainer can take all of these actions without
guessing:

1. add the missing rationale in the `Emergency grants` policy section, adjacent to
   the temporary cap, so the policy is the single authoritative home;
2. record that winter-shelter demand has outgrown the ordinary limit as the cause
   of the temporary $5,000 cap;
3. use the policy rather than meeting chat, meeting minutes, or the newsletter as
   the rationale home; the response need not explicitly discuss those rejected
   alternatives; and
4. avoid invented or restated content that changes, contradicts, or competes with
   the owned causal rationale, insertion action, or authoritative policy home.

Equivalent human-readable locations and causal wording pass.
A short reference to the affected temporary cap is not duplicate rationale.
Other invented policy details or unnecessary repetition are task-fidelity notes
when they leave the owned rationale, insertion action, and authoritative home clear
and unchanged.
Requested labels or formatting are task-fidelity notes only unless their absence
makes the policy change or insertion location ambiguous.

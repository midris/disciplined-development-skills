# CW-03: Whole access-link guide — evaluator only

**Status:** Reviewed scoring approved for commit/push; collection requires separate approval.
Test category / supporting purpose: **Effectiveness**.
Task: [approved prompt](../routine/prompt.md).
Apply the [prospective scoring rules](../../cw-runbook.md#prospective-scoring).
Baseline anchors: Local/global compression; anti-over-trimming of recaps, warnings and orientation.

## Preservation (CW-I1)

- Preserve a single-use URL expiring 30 minutes after issue, opening one recipient's report without an account, issued by an administrator from the report page and sent only to the intended recipient.
- Preserve checking the address against the request before sending, treating the report-page address as a suggestion, and resolving a mismatch with the requester before issuing. Send directly, not to a shared team mailbox.
- Preserve recording report ID and issue time without the URL, including the reason: anyone reading the ticket could open the report.
- Distinguish displaying the report name from successfully opening the report: only the latter consumes the link. A prior network failure does not consume it or extend expiry; closing a successfully opened report does not restore it. Preserve the timing lesson of the 11:00/11:25/11:30 example, whether through that example or equally clear explanation.
- Troubleshooting distinguishes used/expired links needing a new administrator-issued link from an unused, unexpired link that can be retried. Preserve checking prior success and elapsed time against the ticket.
- Replacement sending still requires confirming the recipient despite forwarded conversations, direct delivery, and recording the new issue time without the replacement URL. Preserve the usable sending recap and route to troubleshooting.

## Economy (CW-I2)

- Remove content-free guide/section announcements and other demonstrated local or distant waste. Repeated expiry, recipient checks and no-URL warnings may support lookup or retention; do not fail them solely for occurring in multiple sections.

## Reading and task notes

Readability focus: staff can follow first issuance and recovery without confusing expiry, consumption, recipient confirmation or ticket handling. Judge the whole guide, not isolated sentences.

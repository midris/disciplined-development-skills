# CW-17 routine: generate requested depth — evaluator only

Test category / supporting purpose: **Effectiveness**.
Primary purpose: respect an explicit request for a detailed response when CW is loaded, rather than substituting a fact-only outline.
Apply the [routine-suite assessment rules](../../cw-runbook.md#routine-suite-assessment).

| Invariant | Criterion and pass/fail boundary |
|---|---|
| CW-I1/I3 | Explain the ordered arrival/intake, inspection, acceptance/token issue, sorting, choice and payment process. Preserve clean/wearable acceptance, unaccepted items staying with the owner, size-labeled tables, and payment to the pickup volunteer before an item leaves. |
| CW-I1/I3 | Explain the queue separation causally: donation checking can take time without delaying people choosing items. Merely naming two queues or repeating the timetable fails this explanatory requirement. |
| CW-I1/I3 | Explain 13:00 opening, the 14:00 intake cutoff versus 15:00 pickup cutoff, and what each cutoff stops. Do not suggest a donation can enter after 14:00 just because pickup remains open. |
| CW-I1/I3 | Work through the three-acceptable-items example: inspection/acceptance earns three tokens; describe choosing items and paying one token per item before departure, with at most three purchases from those earned tokens and no obligation to spend all three. The answer must connect actions to the outcome; “three items = three tokens” alone fails. |
| CW-I1/I3 | Work through the one-token-at-15:00 example: pickup has ended, the token has expired, no item/cash/future credit is due, and remaining accepted clothing goes to the community cupboard rather than being reclaimed by donors. Explain why an unfinished visit does not extend the cutoff. |
| CW-I1 | Add no unsupported fees, eligibility rules, locations, donation exceptions or guarantees. Illustrative choices grounded in the supplied rules are allowed. |

The steps, causal explanation and worked examples are requested depth, not padding.
Their absence fails the indicated semantic criterion; do not count the same omission again under task fidelity.
Response-only delivery and absence of file writes are task fidelity.
There is no minimum word count, required heading scheme, or requirement to reproduce the notes.
CW-I2 removal is N/A because no padded draft is supplied and the explicit detailed-response boundary is the purpose.
Neither concision nor the model's self-report establishes whether it internally applied or withheld a method.
A short answer can pass if it supplies the complete explanations; a long factual list without them fails.

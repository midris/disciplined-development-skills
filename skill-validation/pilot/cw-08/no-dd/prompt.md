## Community arts grants

This section explains the rules for community arts grants. Applicants must be 501(c)(3) organizations with annual budgets below $2 million. Organizations without 501(c)(3) status may apply through an eligible fiscal sponsor; see Appendix A for sponsor requirements. Applications are due by 5:00 p.m. ET on October 15. The deadline for applications is October 15 at 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time, and late applications are not reviewed. To request an application accommodation, email `access@communityarts.example` at least five business days before the deadline. Denied applicants may appeal within 10 calendar days; see Appeals for the required form. After the appeal decision, the decision is final.

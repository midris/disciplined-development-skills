## Community arts grants

This section explains the rules for community arts grants.
Applicants must be 501(c)(3) organizations with annual budgets below $2 million.
Organizations without 501(c)(3) status may apply through an eligible fiscal sponsor; see Appendix A for sponsor requirements.
Applications are due by 5:00 p.m. ET on October 15.
The deadline for applications is October 15 at 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time, and late applications are not reviewed.
To request an application accommodation, email `access@communityarts.example` at least five business days before the deadline.
Denied applicants may appeal within 10 calendar days; see Appeals for the required form.
After the appeal decision, the decision is final.

## Appendix A: Fiscal sponsors

An eligible fiscal sponsor is a 501(c)(3) organization with an annual budget below $2 million.
The sponsor signs the application and receives the grant funds on behalf of the applicant.
Include the signed sponsorship agreement with the application.

## Appeals

Use the appeal form attached to the denial email and reply to that email with the completed form within 10 calendar days of the denial email's date.
State which eligibility or review rule you believe was applied incorrectly.
The appeal decision is final; a second appeal is not available.

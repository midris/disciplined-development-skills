# CW-08: Applicant handbook with usable destinations — evaluator only

**Status:** Reviewed scoring approved for commit/push; collection requires separate approval.
Test category / supporting purpose: **Effectiveness**.
Task: [approved prompt](prompt.md).
Apply the [prospective scoring rules](../../cw-runbook.md#prospective-scoring).
Baseline anchors: Core test; non-development scope; anti-over-trimming of exceptions and navigation.

## Preservation (CW-I1)

- Preserve 501(c)(3) eligibility and annual budgets below $2 million, including the exception allowing organizations without that status to apply through an eligible fiscal sponsor. Do not change below to at most.
- Preserve the October 15 deadline at 5:00 p.m. ET and no review of late applications. Preserve requesting an application accommodation by emailing access@communityarts.example at least five business days beforehand.
- Preserve sponsor eligibility, the sponsor signing the application and receiving funds for the applicant, and inclusion of the signed sponsorship agreement.
- Preserve the appeal route: use the form attached to the denial email, reply with it within 10 calendar days of that email's date, and identify the eligibility/review rule believed misapplied. Preserve finality and no second appeal.
- Applicants can find the sponsor requirements and appeal instructions. Original Appendix A/Appeals labels may change if their lookup function survives; do not add an invented form location or submission route.

## Economy (CW-I2)

- Remove the content-free policy announcement. Assess repeated deadline/finality wording by its reader function; remove repetition only when it supplies neither information nor useful framing.
- An overview warning can help applicants decide or navigate, and a reminder beside the appeal instructions can help them act correctly. Neither placement nor repeated facts alone establish waste; apply the shared padding-failure check.

Calibration: the [retained CW-08 pair](/private/tmp/skilltest-cw-policy-w_12rbzo/summary.md#pair-comparisons) illustrates two valid approaches: no-DD repeats appeal timing/finality in the overview and detailed procedure, while current-DD uses an overview pointer and keeps the details in Appeals.
Both preserve useful reader function; neither layout is required, and this example does not exempt all repetition from assessment.

## Reading and task notes

Readability focus: a sponsored applicant or denied applicant can identify the correct next action, route and deadline without confusing calendar and business days.

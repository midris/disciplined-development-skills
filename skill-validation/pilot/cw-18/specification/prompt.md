# Neighborhood clothing swap

This guide explains how volunteers help at the neighborhood clothing swap.
The following sections describe the event from arrival through closing.

## Arrival and intake

Doors open at 13:00.
Intake accepts donations until 14:00; pickup ends at 15:00.
An intake volunteer checks each donated item and accepts only clean, wearable items.
Unaccepted items remain with their owner.
Each accepted item earns one token, so the number of tokens issued equals the number of accepted items.

## Choosing and paying

After intake, a volunteer sorts accepted items onto size-labeled tables.
Intake and pickup use separate queues so checking donations does not hold up people who are choosing items.
Each item taken from a table costs one token, handed to the pickup volunteer before the item leaves.
There is no requirement to use every earned token.
To put this another way, every item taken costs one token and the token must be handed over before the item leaves.

## A visit with three acceptable items

Someone arriving with three acceptable items first has them checked at intake and receives three tokens when all three are accepted.
They can then choose up to three items, giving the pickup volunteer one token for each before taking it away.
If they choose only two items, they have one token left and do not have to choose a third.
This example illustrates how three accepted items provide three tokens to spend.

## Closing and unfinished visits

Tokens can be spent only at this event.
At 15:00, unused tokens expire and cannot be exchanged for cash or reserved for another swap.
A person with one token at 15:00 cannot use it to take an item after pickup closes; having started a visit earlier does not extend the deadline.
Volunteers box the remaining accepted clothing for the community cupboard, and donors do not retrieve their original accepted items.
Unused tokens have no cash value and cannot be saved for a later swap.

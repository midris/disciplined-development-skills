# CW-04: Structure without over-sectioning — evaluator only

**Status:** Reviewed scoring approved for commit/push; collection requires separate approval.
Test category / supporting purpose: **Effectiveness**.
Task: [approved prompt](prompt.md).
Apply the [prospective scoring rules](../../cw-runbook.md#prospective-scoring).
Baseline anchors: Over-sectioning pattern; anti-over-trimming of navigation and orientation.

## Preservation (CW-I1)

- Preserve expiration after 20 minutes of inactivity, warning two minutes before expiration, and mouse/keyboard/touch input resetting the timer. Preserve clearing the warning through that input and signing in again after expiration.
- A reader looking up a warning or recovering after expiration can find the relevant action. Flattening into a dense paragraph is not automatically better; preserving useful headings is valid.

## Economy (CW-I2)

- Remove self-announcing lead-ins and unnecessary subdivisions that add navigation/reading steps without helping this short page. Retaining a heading or a repeated input rule is not itself waste: identify the needless structure or wording and its lack of function.

## Reading and task notes

Readability focus: distinguish ordinary activity, warning response and post-expiration recovery. No prescribed number or depth of headings applies.

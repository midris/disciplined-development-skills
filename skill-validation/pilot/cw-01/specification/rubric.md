## Preservation (CW-I1)

- Preserve queued, running, complete and failed as the four export states; completed exports have a download link and failed exports an error code. Do not merge those outcomes or invent another state.

## Economy (CW-I2)

- Remove content-free announcement of the section and any restatement that serves no reader function. Assess the repeated state/outcome explanations in context rather than banning repeated terminology.

## Reading and task notes

Readability focus: can a reader quickly identify the states and the distinct complete/failed outputs?

# Returning borrowed keys

Return each borrowed key to the desk that issued it before that desk closes.
The east desk closes at 17:00; the west desk closes at 18:00.
Taking an east-desk key to the west desk does not extend its return deadline.

If you cannot reach the issuing desk before closing, call the coordinator using the number on your key tag before the deadline.
The coordinator will arrange a handoff; leaving a voicemail does not confirm one.
Keep the key until the coordinator confirms who will receive it and where.

Do not put keys through the letter slot: it opens into a public corridor, not the locked office.
For example, someone returning an east-desk key at 17:30 must already have arranged a handoff, even though the west desk is still open.

Work read-only within the supplied fixture.
Do not edit files, change Git state, inspect outside the fixture, use the network, or dispatch agents.

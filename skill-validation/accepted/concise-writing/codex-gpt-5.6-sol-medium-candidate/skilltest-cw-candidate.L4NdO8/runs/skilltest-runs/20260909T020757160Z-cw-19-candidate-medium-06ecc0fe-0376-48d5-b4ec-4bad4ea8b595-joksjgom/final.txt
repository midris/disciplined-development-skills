## Orders schema cutover

### Preflight and readiness

Run `./bin/schema-cutover preflight --plan orders-v3 --json`. Attach the JSON output, which records the pre-cutover snapshot ID, to change ticket `CHG-4821`.

Promotion is ready only after both conditions hold throughout the same uninterrupted 15-minute window:

- Database replica lag is at most 2 seconds.
- The dual-write mismatch rate is below 0.1%.

Before promotion, stop if preflight fails, replica lag exceeds 2 seconds, or the mismatch rate reaches 0.1% or higher. Post `BLOCKED` with a link to `CHG-4821` in `#schema-cutover` and tag the database incident commander.

### Promotion

After the readiness window passes, post the `CHG-4821` link in `#schema-cutover` and wait for the database incident commander to reply `GO`. Only then run:

`./bin/schema-cutover promote --plan orders-v3`

Do not add `--force`; it skips the readiness and `GO` checks and can expose partially backfilled rows.

### Recovery boundary

In-place rollback is allowed only until the application accepts its first write containing an `orders-v3`-only field. That write is the irreversible in-place rollback boundary.

After that boundary, do not run:

`./bin/schema-cutover rollback --plan orders-v3`

The old schema cannot represent the write. Only the database incident commander—not the release engineer—may decide whether to continue forward or restore the pre-cutover snapshot recorded in `CHG-4821`.

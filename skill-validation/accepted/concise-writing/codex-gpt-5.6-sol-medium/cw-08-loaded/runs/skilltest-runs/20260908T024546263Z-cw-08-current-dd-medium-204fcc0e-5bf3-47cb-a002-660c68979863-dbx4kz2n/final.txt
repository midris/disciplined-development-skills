## Community arts grants

Applicants must be 501(c)(3) organizations with annual budgets below $2 million. Organizations without 501(c)(3) status may apply through an eligible fiscal sponsor; see Appendix A for sponsor requirements.

Applications are due by 5:00 p.m. ET on October 15. Late applications are not reviewed. For an accommodation, email `access@communityarts.example` at least five business days before the deadline.

Denied applicants may appeal within 10 calendar days; see Appeals for the required form. Appeal decisions are final.

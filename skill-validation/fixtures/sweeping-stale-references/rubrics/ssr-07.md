# SSR-07 evaluator-withheld rubric

PASS only if the proposed replacement comment uses the new session-setting name or
prose form while preserving both causal content: the partner rejects longer sessions,
and accepted-tradeoff content: more frequent refreshes are accepted to preserve
partner compatibility. Generic compatibility language alone is insufficient.

Grade preservation of causal, constraint, rationale, and accepted-tradeoff meaning;
do not grade exact prose or formatting.

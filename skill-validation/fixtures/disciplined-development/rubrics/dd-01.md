## 2. Loading versus child action

Listing a companion under `Repository companions to load now` establishes required loading.
When its detailed action trigger is absent, silence is an acceptable no-op.

Fail if the response:

- omits or prematurely loads a companion;
- positively directs absent-trigger child work; or
- treats absence of immediate child action as making required loading optional.

A may discuss recording the selected architecture after selection, but must not invent rationale for an unselected option.

## 3. Requested ownership seams

A, B, E, and H must use `-`; do not require incidental ownership narration there.

C must state that reviewers emit verdicts while the orchestrator or user accepts gate passage, performs smoke, invokes branch finishing, and opens the PR.

D must assign a cheap/fast model to the one-file complete-text task, a standard model to the multi-file integration task, and the most capable available model to the architecture/design task. It must also state that the orchestrator scopes dispatches, verifies returned work, and retains the parent gates; development subagents implement only bounded work, report due parent gates rather than taking them over, and do not dispatch further subagents.

F must state that reviewers emit findings and verdicts, the orchestrator or user accepts gate passage, and the applicable child owns remediation method.

G must surface the conflict for clarification without interpretation, technical evaluation, remediation, or implementation; the orchestrator or user decides the governing meaning before work continues.

Equivalent concise wording is accepted.
Do not require child procedures or repetition of absent actions beyond these boundaries.

## 4. DD-02 boundary

Do not require DD-01 to state the Gate 1-5 child procedures, complete Gate 5 lifecycle, or their sequence.
Those are DD-02 observables.

## 5. Decision

PASS only if all eight rows are present and Sections 1-4 pass.
FAIL on any wrong mode, methodology set, companion load set, requested exclusion, or requested ownership seam.

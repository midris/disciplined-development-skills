# DD-02 evaluator-withheld rubric

Score the complete response as an executable parent-orchestration plan.
Grade whether it chooses effective actions at the right time, preserves their order and owners, blocks invalid transitions, and requires truthful parent-owned records.
Do not grade exact wording, table shape, column labels, checkpoint or rule numbering, gate/principle names, or status vocabulary.
Record those rendering or terminology differences as advisory notes only.
Do not grade child names, loading, procedure details, or child-output quality.

## Behavioral contract

1. At session start, the response requires a fresh read of every supplied governing source, surfaces the plan/spec spelling conflict to the decision owner without silently choosing, and prevents planning or implementation until the sources are read and the owner resolves the conflict.
   It recognizes that factual claims need current support without being scored on the child research procedure, source ranking, or disclosure quality.
2. After the owner selects `--format json`, the response requires complete written and signed scope before implementation planning or coding.
   That record reconciles the spelling decision, preserves applicable project constraints, and records the intentional XML deferral, its reason, and its consequence for consumers.
3. After scope approval, any delegation remains bounded and the orchestrator retains scope control, returned-work acceptance, and later review/commit/PR boundaries.
   Production editing cannot begin until a regression test is written, run, and observed failing for the expected missing behavior; a returned report or green result cannot substitute for that evidence or for inspection of the actual returned diff.
4. When the green candidate returns, the response first identifies the `output_mode` to `format` schema-key rename as outside the signed scope.
   It rejects and removes that work or stops for decision-owner resolution and revised signed scope before verification, reconciliation, acceptance, or commit involving the rename.
   Once the candidate conforms to signed scope, completion still requires an actual CLI invocation with its result pasted as direct evidence.
   Before commit, it requires an effective reference sweep that finds and reconciles the applicable code, schema, examples, and documentation, plus a concise and truthful summary in the skill-declared durable destination: the commit body's `References swept:` section.
   One green commit contains the test, implementation, and reconciled references.
5. Before PR creation, whole-repository self-review must find the orphaned `confirm_overwrite()` safeguard even though it is outside the current diff.
   The response cannot pass self-review or silently implement the safeguard while its scope is unresolved; the decision owner must authorize revised signed scope or an explicit governing disposition before any remediation.
   Any resulting change invalidates the affected review/smoke work and causes the sequence to restart from the earliest affected stage.
   The ordered acceptance path is whole-repository self-review to a blocking-finding-free PASS, fresh external review over the same scope to PASS, smoke with exact commands and results recorded in the Gate 2 completion/acceptance record, then branch finishing and PR creation.
   Reviewers only emit findings and verdicts; the remediation-method owner handles fixes; only the orchestrator or user accepts passage, performs smoke, invokes branch finishing, or opens the PR.
6. Every transition is fail-closed: the required action or artifact exists before the blocked action proceeds, and phase or governing-artifact changes trigger a fresh read of the actual sources relevant to the next action.

## Decision

PASS only when the complete response makes every required action, timing constraint, order dependency, owner boundary, blocked transition, and truthful record above clear enough to execute without guessing.
FAIL on any missing, ineffective, incorrectly timed, misordered, wrongly owned, or ambiguous required behavior.
A correct label cannot cure wrong or missing behavior, and a label or rendering difference cannot fail otherwise clear and correct behavior.
Incidental child procedure cannot cure a missing parent observable and is not independently scored.

## Criteria

### T1: Deliver a shorter usable plan

Basis: Task requirement.
Coverage: [Suite extension](../../../protocol.md#suite-extension).
Dimension: functional
Applies to: `original`, `control`
Judgment unit: Deliver a shorter usable plan in this execution.
Required evidence: Compare the entire final plan and handoff with the original plan and request.
Met: The plan removes redundant prose. A reader can identify both daily commitments as in launch and both weekly commitments as deliberately outside launch. The guide handoff and first-month adoption review retain their meaning.
Not met: A weekly item disappears without a recoverable status, a weekly alert still ships, a daily item disappears, an unrelated commitment is dropped, or the original verbose plan survives with only a chat summary.
Insufficient evidence: Missing or incomplete project, task, response or applicable replay prevents judgment.
Alternatives: Credit any solution preserving the supplied meaning and intended action.
Consequence: Record the specific loss for the intended reader or the failed task obligation.
Overlap: Report a shared cause once across affected criteria.

## Boundary rulings

For T1, state the status of each original deliverable from the plan alone. “Their weekly versions” can identify both when it follows the daily reconciliation and alert commitments. “Weekly reporting” alone may leave alerts unaccounted for. Record an unidentified item under T1. Assess the reason, trigger and consequence under R1. If all weekly material vanishes, both criteria miss from one deletion.

Read headings and surrounding sentences together. “Deferred until Finance agrees” can accurately name a prerequisite. Under a deferral heading with no commitment to ship, credit it with a precision caveat. A delivery verb, dated sequence or list of committed follow-ups can turn the same words into a promise. Judge their meaning together. A commitment to ship on agreement gives Operations a delivery milestone the team never agreed to. The same materiality rule applies to the guard case: there the reader still knew when to reopen an internal design choice. Here an invented milestone can change a launch plan.

“Support may need to compare daily reports” can inherit the affected-week condition from the immediately preceding cross-period discussion. Credit that reading with a precision caveat if a broader reading is also plausible. If the plan directs Support to compare reports routinely, the obligation has changed. If the possible Support workload disappears entirely, R1 misses an operational consequence even when the reason and trigger survive. Judge the first-month adoption review independently of the Finance trigger.

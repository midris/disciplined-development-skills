### Reporting differences

Local-change exposes a repeatable contract difference: both originals use the required `References swept: n/a` line, while both candidates produce a positive inventory despite correctly limiting the repair to one file (orders 17–20).
The frozen criterion requires the negative form after establishing locality, including when searches find valid near-matches elsewhere.
The candidate instead says “If all mutable-tree matches are in the changed file”; unchanged or unrelated matches elsewhere can therefore lead to a positive inventory.
This is an observed regression against the accepted local-change contract, not a functional repair failure or proof that the subjects ignored the candidate’s own wording.
The case was fixed before candidate comparison; its criterion has not been relaxed to accommodate the candidate.

The originals have three clear counting inconsistencies: Shiv order 1 claims seven paths while listing eight; Shiv order 4 has category totals that disagree with its entries; initiating-change order 16 claims ten paths where its complete inventory has nine.
The candidates do not show those inconsistencies in the corresponding ordinary cases.
Two repetitions per condition cannot establish a dependable counting advantage.

Other P3 mismatches must not be promoted into established skill defects.
All four Shiv accounts label preserved negative assertions “intentionally stale” instead of the frozen “false positive” category; this is the previously disclosed classification boundary.
Ordinary semantic-delivery orders 9–10 and all four pressure runs count historical phrases/lines rather than the case’s single semantic block; pressure order 21 also counts two README phrases rather than one block.
Every required meaning is preserved or repaired, and the skill does not define this block unit.
The frozen judgments remain visible, but these differences do not independently justify authoring changes or a claim that pressure damaged performance.
Positive native cases assess F1–F3 and D1 only; their accounts are not silently added to the P3 comparison.

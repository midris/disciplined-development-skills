## Criteria

### P3: Traceable and truthful sweep account

Basis: Current [original skill](../../../../cases/skill-original/SKILL.md), [protocol contract](../../../../protocol.md#behavioral-contract-and-consumers), and explicit [task](../../../../cases/large-sweep-account/task.md); severity follows [policy 3](../../../../cases/large-sweep-account/assessment-policy.txt).
Coverage: O4
Dimension: procedural
Applies to: `original`, `candidate`, `finalist`
Judgment unit: Complete REPORT.md against the settled supplied inventory
Required evidence: Saved report, supplied inventory and any subject verification events.
Met: Every required reference remains traceable with correct disposition, locations and counts; grouping is by path/outcome; verification distinguishes supplied facts from performed checks.
Not met: Required evidence is omitted, mixed, counted incorrectly or falsely represented as executed verification.
Insufficient evidence: Missing, truncated or conflicting evidence prevents deciding the required behavior; report the specific gap rather than infer a pass or failure.
Alternatives: Tables or bullets; accurate split groups allowed; no compulsory redundant totals; necessary detail can exceed the normal length preference.
Consequence: Report a non-blocking procedural defect or insufficient evidence; this cannot change a functional outcome or justify a repeat by itself.
Overlap: No functional rollup: this diagnostic measures accounting only.

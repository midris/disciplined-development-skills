## Criteria

### D1: Avoid unnecessary SSR loading

Basis: SSR's changing-fact/reviewer-reference triggers and the [CW testing model](../../../../../../plans/specs/2026-09-11-model-driven-skill-testing-framework.md#cw-testing-model).
Coverage: native applicability boundary during test-only verification of a completed rename
Dimension: procedural
Applies to: `original`, `candidate`, `finalist`
Judgment unit: observed SSR selection/body-delivery/application sequence during this task
Required evidence: qualified availability and complete ordered native tool calls, body responses and task actions.
Met: no SSR Skill invocation, targeted body read or application occurs; catalog/name/description exposure is allowed.
Not met: SSR is invoked, a targeted read delivers body content (partial or complete), or its sweep/accounting procedure is applied despite the test-only task.
Insufficient evidence: missing/conflicting capture or ambiguous body provenance prevents establishing the relevant event; identify the gap, not an assumed private decision.
Alternatives: any effective execution of the requested tests; catalog inspection or reading ordinary project material does not itself select SSR. Record incidental bulk exposure and denied/undelivered read attempts separately; neither proves a targeted body load.
Consequence: report surplus selection/loading as a procedural caution, with task damage or unauthorized work separately described; no functional SSR success/failure denominator exists here and no retry follows automatically.
Overlap: a single surplus-load episode can include invocation, reading and sweep actions; do not count it as several failed executions.

## Criteria

### P3: Useful count-free account

Basis: Owner-approved count-free compact Commit account; SSR-count-free-1 supersedes exact-count requirements only.
Coverage: [Protocol reporting simplification](../../../../protocol.md#reporting-simplification-2026-10-31).
Dimension: procedural
Applies to: `finalist`
Judgment unit: Required case-specific references and their meaning, paths, locations, changes or preservation reasons; no numeric counting unit.
Required evidence: Complete commit body (REPORT.md for large-sweep-account), source/final artifacts and ordered trace against the inherited case's required reference scope.
Met: References swept: accounts for required paths with useful locations and correct updates or preservation explanations, grouped by the same path/outcome; changes use faithful old → new encodings. The local negative form is valid only when all mutable-tree matches are in the changed file. Reporting-only tasks truthfully distinguish supplied state from work performed.
Not met: Missing required paths, changes or preservation reasons; materially incorrect dispositions/locations; cross-path or mixed-outcome grouping; unjustified local negative form; or unsupported claims of performed repair/checks.
Insufficient evidence: Missing captured account or context prevents judging coverage; an inspectable absent required account is not met. Preserve the inherited Shiv negative-assertion category uncertainty when it is the only issue.
Alternatives: Counts may be omitted. Optional numerical errors are descriptive observations, not exact-count failures. Supplemental references outside the fixed required case scope do not create new criteria.
Consequence: Reporting defects remain separate and non-blocking unless they also establish a functional failure; no replacement attempt follows.
Overlap: Functional outcome, preservation and committed completeness remain F1–F3; ordered search and commit grouping remain P1/P2.

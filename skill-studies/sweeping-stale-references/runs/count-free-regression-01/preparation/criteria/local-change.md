Required evidence: Complete commit body (REPORT.md for large-sweep-account), source/final artifacts and ordered trace against the inherited case's required reference scope.

Overlap: Functional outcome, preservation and committed completeness remain F1–F3; ordered search and commit grouping remain P1/P2.

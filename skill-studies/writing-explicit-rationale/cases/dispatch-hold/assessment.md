# Apply a shared dispatch policy

## Identity and purpose

Format version: `1`
Study ID: `writing-explicit-rationale`
Case ID: `dispatch-hold`
Definition version: `1`
Status: prepared for offline qualification
Purpose and mechanism: Two planners implement one policy already explained in the spec. A routine title edit accompanies the consequential change.

## Inputs and setup

Supply [the task](task.md) and [starting project](fixture/). Keep the case card, worked patch and qualification materials with the controller. Use identical task and project bytes across compared arms. This extension is prepared for an explicitly loaded original versus candidate comparison.

## Rules and evidence

Apply the [whole-output policy](../../protocol.md#assessment-policy) and the [reader-first procedure](../README.md#reader-first-check). Read the entire project and handoff against the original inputs. Accept alternate wording, layout and implementation. The [worked repair](example.patch) illustrates one passing solution.
 The [private replay](replay.py) checks observable behavior and the two titles. Judge meaning by reading the project. A reader may find the policy without a literal link.

## Criteria

### T1: Update the policy and both consumers

Basis: Task requirement.
Coverage: [Suite extension](../../protocol.md#suite-extension).
Dimension: functional
Applies to: `original`, `candidate`
Judgment unit: Update the policy and both consumers in this execution.
Required evidence: Compare the original and final spec, both public lookups and screen titles. Run the private replay on a disposable final tree.
Met: Both planners apply both hold states to new trips and preserve already-loaded assignments. Ready parcels stay eligible. The spec covers the new state and the requested title is correct.
Not met: A planner removes a loaded parcel, accepts a held parcel for a new trip, or the spec or title misses the requested update.
Insufficient evidence: Missing or incomplete project, task, response or applicable replay prevents judgment.
Alternatives: Credit any solution preserving the supplied meaning and intended action.
Consequence: Record the specific loss for the intended reader or the failed task obligation.
Overlap: Report a shared cause once across affected criteria.

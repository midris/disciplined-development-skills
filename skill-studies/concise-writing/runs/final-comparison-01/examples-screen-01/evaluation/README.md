# Short examples comparison output review

The [protocol](../../../../protocol.md) owns current scope, accounting and the conditional body decision.
The two approved Opus5.5medium sessions each review six fresh outputs, three per body from one subject provider.
The active assessor is the Codex controller; reviewers are a different model family relative to that assessor and the same family as Claude subjects.
Condition labels are visible; independent review is not blinded review.

The frozen inputs include the complete source, task, setup, criteria, policy, interpretation, counts, six outputs and a separate prior-assessment file.
Skill bodies, descriptions, hypotheses, historical outputs/reviews and raw subject traces are excluded.
The first pass must cover every complete output before access to prior scores.
The native tools are Read, Glob and Grep; no shell, skill tool or slash command is available.
Complete-read delivery, first-pass ordering, model/runtime identity, protected bytes and retained inventories are audited separately from manually checking semantic completeness.
Three repetitions support diagnostic comparisons, not reliability or equivalence estimates.

One subject attempt failed on expired OAuth before inference; its full index entry and charge remain recorded.
The owner approved one unchanged replacement from the retry pool.
Only the twelve valid outputs enter the two review packets; excluding the invalid setup from functional denominators does not remove its provenance or charge.
The [resumption record](../preparation/resumption/resumption.json) and [manual aggregate-audit rationale](../preparation/resumption/README.md) retain that boundary and the no-retry batch-assessment checker's limitation.

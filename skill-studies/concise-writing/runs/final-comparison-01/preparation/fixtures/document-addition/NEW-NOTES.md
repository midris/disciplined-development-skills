# Additional Search preview requirements

Before sending an invitation, Nia must also record a named fallback contact for that team.
The existing consent, ability-to-switch-back, timeout and Thursday-approval checks still apply.
Invitations remain opt-in and the proposed expansion is still four teams; these points repeat the existing policy rather than replacing it.

While a team uses the preview, a report of a potentially incorrect search result requires pausing the preview for that affected team and retaining the query and returned result in the restricted incident record.
This precaution does not imply that the report has already been confirmed as a defect.
Other participating teams may continue under the existing policy unless the investigation identifies a broader problem.
The affected team can resume only after Omar has investigated and Nia approves resumption.

The seven-day review must include any pauses and their investigation outcomes alongside timeout data and feedback.
A successful review still requires a separate decision before further expansion; it does not authorize enabling the preview by default.

# Final-comparison Opus review design

The [comparison design](../../../drafts/baseline-iteration/final-comparison/README.md) and [protocol](../../../protocol.md) govern allocation and authority.
The [twenty-packet schedule](review-schedule.json) contains six outputs per case/provider packet, preserving explicit/native strata and all three repetitions of both bodies.
No review configuration, packet or result is represented as frozen or collected yet.

Use actual Opus 5.5 at requested medium effort in fresh contexts with qualified read-only native Read/Glob/Grep, no shell, skills or slash commands.
Require complete source/task/notes/code and neutral setup/explicit-load wrapper, applicable criteria/policy/interpretation, all six output artifacts and mechanical/length observations.
For N3 supply the complete assistant streams and score only numeric and literal-reply task observations, not F1-F3.
For comment cases provide token/syntax results; never execute subject output.
The active controller alone judges native D1 from ordered traces, including misses or unnecessary loads.

Keep skill bodies, hypotheses and raw traces out of quality packets; labels remain visible and the active Codex assessor has authoring exposure.
Map every per-run factor/task judgment and quoted evidence to its exact run ID, condition and repetition before reading prior scores.
First-pass capture, complete native Read delivery and absence of early prior exposure must be audited; preserve any later correction and its timing without replacing independent evidence.
No automatic retry, split, model fallback or capacity transfer is authorized.
Freeze exact packet/controller/input identities only after subject assessment and prior to the first review.

The [protocol](../../../../protocol.md) owns current authority and accounting.
The two [frozen input groups](inputs.json) each contain six new subject outputs, three per unchanged body, with complete factual sources, setup wrapper, criteria, policy, interpretation and descriptive counts.
[Prompt](prompt.md) fixes the whole-artifact judgments and independent-first-pass-before-prior order.
Skill bodies, previous reviews, candidate hypotheses and raw subject traces are excluded.

The active assessor is Codex with authoring/development exposure; Opus 5.5 medium supplies the owner-requested different-family review relative to it, while sharing the Claude subjects' family.
Condition labels are visible, so reviews are independent but not blinded.
The canonical private controller pins every input and subject report, restricts native tools to Read/Glob/Grep under the qualified read-only profile, requires manual audit clearance before advancing, and allows exactly two claimed reviewer sessions.
The [ledger](evaluator-run-index.json) retains every started invocation and charge; no automatic retry, split or fallback is authorized.

For each review, verify complete delivery before its first judgments and prior-score access, actual model/CLI/tools, protected bytes and inventories.
Read every factor, quotation and comparison manually before clearing semantic completeness; retain disagreements and post-prior changes separately.
Three repetitions are descriptive; equal passes do not prove equivalent quality or support reliability claims.
The owner makes the next continuation and adoption decision after reviewing the evidence.

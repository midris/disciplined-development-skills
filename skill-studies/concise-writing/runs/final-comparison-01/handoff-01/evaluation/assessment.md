## Decision boundary and limitations

This is an exposed development case without a contemporaneous no-CW arm; it compares complete combinations rather than measuring incremental skill contribution.
Three repetitions and complementary condition orders support a diagnostic, not population reliability, equivalence, randomization or a causal body-effect claim.
The bodies remain 869 original and 980 revised complete-file words, a 111-word difference under the same description.
The owner's shorter-SKILL.md tie-break provisionally favors the original where consequential effectiveness and quality are judged equivalent; local cleanup differences and remaining suite coverage still belong in the owner's decision.
Comment evidence remains a separate five-repetition case, not additional handoff observations.
Stop at this twelve-subject/two-review checkpoint; any top-up, next case, body/description edit or adoption needs the owner's bounded decision.

A five-repetition handoff continuation would add eight subjects and two updated ten-output reviews, reusing these twelve outputs without new subject charges for them.
That is an unapproved next option, not a remaining allocation.

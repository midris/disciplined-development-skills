## What the comparisons show

- **A/B ordinary editing:** most outputs already work, including controls.
  Drafts sometimes make decision framing or navigation clearer, but neither consistently improves the original.
  Claude controls on A twice add an unsupported pilot purpose concerning conditions that remain untested; both assessors fail those artifacts, while disagreeing whether F2 also fails.
  On B, several Claude controls restructure more than the skill outputs, so successful editing cannot be credited to CW alone.
- **C handoff:** the clearest useful CW pattern is removal of the detached mechanism reminder while keeping safeguards at Deployment and Rollback.
  On Claude it is removed by original and targeted 3/3, fuller 2/3, and controls 0/3.
  Opus treats the retained reminder as a weakness rather than an F3 failure; the qualitative benefit survives that disagreement.
  Targeted is roughly comparable to original here; fuller has a retained reminder and one awkward “encouraging-only” phrase.
  One targeted output removes the source's explicit statement that a lease-token rationale is unknown while retaining the unconditional rule twice: this loses transparency content, rather than merely omitting an unscored uncertainty flag, but does not remove the operational safeguard.
- **N1 tightening:** all versions remove obvious padding and preserve decisions and safeguards.
  Passing outputs differ in explicit causal links and navigation; the original sometimes states the pilot-limit reason more consistently, and individual draft outputs organize actions particularly well.
  Neither draft has a consistent quality advantage.
- **N2 writing from notes:** all conditions produce usable layouts, with occasional inferred ownership, deployment-state wording or validation framing.
  Both assessors identify original Claude repetition 3's narrowed staffing gate: “if Ali is unavailable” omits Bea's independent unavailability condition.
  Ordered traces show that run never loaded CW, so it is not evidence of applying the original body.
  The drafts have some stronger individual outputs, but the apparent categorical advantage depends partly on disputed extrapolation thresholds.
- **N4 comments:** all eighteen outputs preserve syntax/executable tokens and replace narration with useful retry/acknowledgement guidance.
  Longer comments are justified by supplied maintenance rationale, rather than failed concision.
  Opus flags one original Codex output's implicit explanation, while other outputs vary in how explicitly they name the duplicate-upload risk.
  Without native controls, output quality cannot establish incremental contribution; most Claude subjects never loaded the body.

The complete supplied SKILL.md sizes are **860 original, 955 targeted and 1,084 fuller words**, including frontmatter and Markdown.
The reviewers did not receive the skill bodies; these counts come from the controller's exact `wc -w` check.
Both drafts are longer, and no recurring improvement across the suite justifies preferring either on current evidence.

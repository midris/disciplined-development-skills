# CW evaluation-guideline application audit

The [protocol](../../protocol.md) owns current action, spending and adoption authority.
This owner-requested audit applies existing criteria; it adds no subject/evaluator calls, skill edits or new readability requirement.

## Conclusion

The written guidelines already require whole-document readability, meaningful comparative quality and preservation of useful framing; they do not optimize word count.
Their application is substantial but not uniformly reliable at disputed semantic/severity boundaries, and the summary given to the owner understated meaningful quality differences among passing artifacts.
The audit confirms one additional owner-resolved scoring correction and makes a recurring layout difference explicit.
The later [holistic reassessment](../holistic-dispute-reassessment-01/README.md) owns the requested follow-up on other disputed judgments; it preserves this audit's scope, inventory and correction mapping.
It does not establish that every previous semantic judgment is correct or that either body rewrite is generally superior.

## What was applied correctly

- Readability was assessed explicitly, not omitted: recorded F3 reasons discuss document sequence, causal connections, scanning, paragraphs, useful recaps, independent operational entry points and consumption burden.
- Length is separate from quality: the N4 reports accept longer comments when they convey maintenance rationale; E additions can grow; the effective-briefing rules permit restraint or an unchanged effective source.
- Useful repetition is distinguished from padding: safety rules at independently used Deployment/Rollback entry points are retained rather than penalized simply for appearing twice.
- Factors are separate: the latest Codex C control repetition 2 is readable in linear order but loses prerequisites at its Deployment entry point, producing an F2 failure rather than an automatic F3 failure.
- Native invocation and artifact quality remain separate, including good comments produced without loading CW and procedural-only numeric edits.
- Both evaluator prompts already require meaningful quality comparisons between passing outputs, complete-context judgments and no automatic word-count win; a missing instruction in those prompts is not the explanation for the discrepancies.

## Comparative quality that threshold counts obscure

In the owner's reviewed repetition-2 group, all three CW-assisted documents are much easier to read than the control, and targeted feels more compact than original and fuller.
That advantage remains meaningful when all four outputs pass; it does not follow from a word-count minimum.

Across the complete latest Codex A set, all three controls place the causal old/new implementation comparison inside an evidence bullet alongside the observations.
All nine assisted outputs give that comparison a separate paragraph or section.
Separating observations from mechanism gives the reader a clearer change of topic and reduces packing of evidence and explanation into one bullet.
This is a recurring layout difference in this exposed stratum, consistent with the owner's observed readability preference; it is not proof that every assisted artifact is superior on every dimension or that the effect generalizes.
The prior Opus review noted another recurring distinction, the explicit purpose of retained evidence, but that observation did not capture this density/layout difference.

The C reread also confirms why quality must be reported beyond one marker: removing a reminder, adding an operational list, separating conditions and preserving entry-point safeguards are different reader effects.
Retaining a section is not automatically a failure, and removing it does not establish overall readability superiority.
The eighteen N4 outputs vary in explanatory explicitness and comment length while all pass active F3; the disputed acknowledgement rationale concerns contextual sufficiency, not a requirement to be shortest.
These observations strengthen the comparison without selecting a universal winning variant.

## Decision implications

The completed scope covers 136 distinct subject outputs: 124 with functional criteria and twelve procedural-only N3 outputs.
All complete Fable functional judgments are passes; this describes its threshold, not a pooled effectiveness rate or proof of equivalence.
Twelve primary sessions and one explicitly approved retry were captured, including the incomplete original E review.
The manual handoff review’s all-pass result was reproduced under the declared isolated conditions.
Read/ordering audits verify complete declared material before emitted judgments and prior-score access; the E correction additionally verifies the task inputs against original configurations.

The review adds useful quality observations but exposes a permissive “main decision survives” threshold across rationale, action-recording and unsupported-conclusion cases.
Accepting some C handoff outputs as readable is defensible; unsupported factual claims or a missing recording action require separate consideration.
The original is qualitatively preferred in Claude A and E; comprehensive outputs sometimes retain more rationale or neutral framing in other cases.
There is no clear overall version preference or demonstrated equivalent consequential quality on this evidence.
Do not choose an editing base by counting model votes or applying the shorter-SKILL.md tie-break to tied Fable passes alone.
These examples distinguish lost requirements from acceptable compression; the protocol owns subsequent authoring decisions and allocations.
Native D1 results remain unchanged and must accompany a later adoption decision; output quality does not resolve selection misses or over-triggering.

### D1: Avoid CW during a numeric-only task

Basis: Owner-selected task and [behavioral contract](../../protocol.md#behavioral-contract-and-consumers).
Coverage: Near-boundary non-prose edit
Dimension: procedural
Applies to: `original`, `candidate-comprehensive`
Judgment unit: Native selection during a numeric-only edit
Required evidence: Qualified catalog availability and complete ordered tool/read events.
Met: The subject does not load CW while performing the numeric-only task; the fixed literal completion requires no language composition.
Not met: The subject loads CW despite a task confined to one numeric value and an exact literal reply.
Insufficient evidence: Missing or conflicting capture prevents establishing the required evidence; identify the gap rather than inferring success or failure.
Alternatives: Equivalent wording, structure and tool use are accepted where they preserve the stated outcome; no hidden preferred answer.
Consequence: Functional outcome is not measured for CW; numeric-edit correctness and unauthorized comment changes are recorded as task observations only.
Overlap: A shared cause may affect multiple criteria but is one execution; report invocation and task outcomes separately.

## What we learned

Over the past week, the six teams submitted 120 searches. Two timed out; both succeeded when retried. We have no evidence of lost documents, but we have not tested searches over more than 50,000 documents. Support received three questions about the new filters and no reports of incorrect results. A quiet support queue does not establish that every result was correct.

The four proposed teams each have fewer than 20,000 documents. Adding them would broaden feedback without crossing the untested large-collection boundary. It would not validate large collections.

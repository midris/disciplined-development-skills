# Controller-only CW boundary checks

These are constructed assessment examples, not subject outputs, calibration statistics or a preferred answer.
Read each complete source and the case card before applying a boundary; a sentence difference prompts inspection of the whole artifact, not an automatic deduction.
The existing [worked prose examples](../qualification.md) continue to apply to A/B and inform C's policy-4 judgments.

These checks distinguish correctness, utility, readability, process and selection without pooling them.
They cannot establish an incremental CW benefit: that requires the planned contemporaneous comparisons, and a control that passes remains a valid observation.
